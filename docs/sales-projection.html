    5. How each stream is calculated

    • Buy and sell (30): a customer sells us a game and we resell it. We pay the buy-back price (cash or credit, reduced by the condition factor). Profit = resale price − what we paid − shrinkage + delivery fee − courier cost − pickup cost, counted only for the share that resells in the month.
    • Trade-in (25): the customer gives a game and takes a game of similar value. We give credit for theirs and they pay the gap in cash. Profit shown is the margin on the item we sell now; the traded-in game is stock that earns more when it resells (shown as "pipeline").
    • Swap (15): two customers exchange games through us. We earn the flat swap fee from each side and pay for pickups and deliveries.
    • Member sales (30): a member lists an item. The buyer pays the seller's price plus our commission (rounded up to $0.50) plus the delivery fee. We pay for collecting from the seller and delivering to the buyer.
    Prices, mix and rates are illustrations using the platform's current default settings. Not financial advice or a guarantee of results. Fixed monthly costs default to an assumed $150 (hosting, packaging, phone) and are editable.
