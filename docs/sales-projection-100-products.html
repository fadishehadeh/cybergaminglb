    6. How each type is calculated

    • Used games (40): bought from customers (cash or credit, reduced by the condition factor) and resold. Profit = sale price − what we paid − shrinkage + delivery fee − courier cost − the cost of collecting from the seller. Every seller pays the $5 pickup from their credit, so pickups cost us little; the real risk is remote items rejected on inspection, where part of the return leg is never recovered.
    • New games (15): sealed copies bought from a supplier at a set margin.
    • Accessories (15): controllers, headsets and cables bought from a supplier at a higher margin.
    • Gift cards (15): digital, so no courier and no delivery fee. Profit is the thin card margin minus expected fraud losses.
    • Member sales (15): the buyer pays the member's price plus our commission (rounded up to $0.50) plus the delivery fee. We pay for collecting and delivering.

    Each product is assigned to a local or remote customer from a fixed random draw, so changing the local share moves the same products between zones.

    Prices, mix and rates are illustrations, not financial advice or a guarantee. Fixed monthly costs default to an assumed $150 and are editable.
